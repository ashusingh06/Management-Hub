Official Qualifier Passing Criteria

Phase 1 (Weeks 1 to 4)

Weekly Assignment Cutoff (Hall Ticket)

To be eligible to appear for the Qualifier Exam in-person, you must submit the graded weekly assignments for weeks 1–4:

  • General Category: Minimum 40% average across best 3 out of 4 weekly assignments in each course.
  • OBC-NCL / EWS: Minimum 35% average.
  • SC / ST / PwD: Minimum 30% average.
Phase 2 (In-Person Exam)

Qualifier Exam Passing Cutoff

In the 4-hour in-person center examination covering all 4 courses, you must satisfy:

  • Subject Cutoff: Minimum 40 marks (40%) in each of the 4 individual subjects (Math 1, CT, English 1, Stats 1).
  • Result: If you score ≥ 40 in all 4 subjects, you are officially Qualified & Admitted to Foundation Level!
  • Once passed, you can register for up to 4 new Foundation Level courses in your first term.
