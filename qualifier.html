Qualifier Weekly Syllabus (Weeks 1 – 4)

Official 4-week qualifier curriculum for all 4 foundation subjects. Click any subject tab to view its week-by-week topic breakdown:

📐

Mathematics for Data Science I

BSMA1001 • 4 Credits • Qualifier Weeks 1 – 4
Open Math 1 Notes & PYQs
WEEK 1 Assignment 1

Set Theory, Relations & Functions

Set Theory - Number system, Sets and their operations, Relations and functions - Relations and their types, Functions and their types

WEEK 2 Assignment 2

Rectangular Coordinate System & Straight Lines

Rectangular coordinate system, Straight Lines - Slope of a line, Parallel and perpendicular lines, Representations of a Line, General equations of a line, Straight-line fit

WEEK 3 Assignment 3

Quadratic Functions & Equations

Quadratic Functions - Quadratic functions, Minima, maxima, vertex, and slope, Quadratic Equations

WEEK 4 Assignment 4

Algebra of Polynomials & Graphs

Algebra of Polynomials - Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division, Algorithms, Graphs of Polynomials - X-intercepts, multiplicities, end behavior, and turning points, Graphing & polynomial creation

📊

Statistics for Data Science I

BSMA1002 • 4 Credits • Qualifier Weeks 1 – 4
Open Stats 1 Notes & PYQs
WEEK 1 Assignment 1

Introduction & Types of Data

Introduction and type of data, Types of data, Descriptive and Inferential statistics, Scales of measurement

WEEK 2 Assignment 2

Describing Categorical Data

Describing categorical data Frequency distribution of categorical data, Best practices for graphing categorical data, Mode and median for categorical variable

WEEK 3 Assignment 3

Describing Numerical Data & Dispersion

Describing numerical data Frequency tables for numerical data, Measures of central tendency - Mean, median and mode, Quartiles and percentiles, Measures of dispersion - Range, variance, standard deviation and IQR, Five number summary

WEEK 4 Assignment 4

Association Between Two Variables

Association between two variables - Association between two categorical variables - Using relative frequencies in contingency tables, Association between two numerical variables - Scatterplot, covariance, Pearson correlation coefficient, Point bi-serial correlation coefficient

Official Qualifier Passing Criteria

Phase 1 • Weeks 1 to 4

Weekly Assignment Cutoff (Hall Ticket)

To be eligible to appear for the in-person Qualifier Exam, you must submit the graded weekly assignments for weeks 1–4:

General Category Min 40% avg (best 3 of 4)
OBC-NCL / EWS Min 35% avg (best 3 of 4)
SC / ST / PwD Min 30% avg (best 3 of 4)
Phase 2 • In-Person Exam

Qualifier Exam Passing Cutoff

In the 4-hour in-person center examination covering all 4 courses, you must satisfy:

Subject Cutoff Min 40 marks (40%) in each subject
Final Result Score ≥ 40 in all 4 subjects = PASS

🎉 Once qualified, you are admitted to IIT Madras BS Foundation Level and can register for up to 4 courses in Term 1.
